Anatomy

Three layers between you and the road

Scroll to take the Vela 2 apart. Each layer does one job, tuned for the 1,400 or so footstrikes in an average 10 km run.

Layer 1 of 3 · Upper

Engineered mono-knit

The knit is tighter across the midfoot for lockdown and opens up over the toes for airflow. A gusseted tongue stays centred on long runs, and the padded collar sits low to clear the Achilles.

  • Upper 62 g
  • 78% recycled polyester
  • Gusseted tongue
Layer 2 of 3 · Midsole

PEBA-blend foam

A full-length slab of supercritical foam, 38 mm at the heel and 30 mm at the forefoot. In lab testing it returns 84% of impact energy and keeps that feel past 600 km.

  • Stack 38 / 30 mm
  • Drop 8 mm
  • Energy return 84%
Layer 3 of 3 · Outsole

Carbon rubber pods

Rubber sits only where runners land: the lateral heel and under the forefoot. The 3 mm lugs grip wet asphalt, and leaving foam bare between them saves 30 g per shoe.

  • Lug depth 3 mm
  • 68 Shore A
  • −30 g vs full coverage
